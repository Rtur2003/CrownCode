Language detection is a crucial component of natural language p rocessing, enabling applications such as
machine translation, sentiment analysis, and information retrieval. This study proposes a novel Bidirectional
Long Short-Term Memory (BiLSTM) model with a Multi-Head Attenti on mechanism to classify text into 
17 languages accurately. A publicly available dataset containing 10,337 text samples is used for training and
evaluation. The proposed model achieves an outstanding accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score of 99.9%, 
surpassing state-of-the-art models. Notably, it attains perfect  precision (100%) for 15 languages. These
findings demonstrate the effectiveness of deep learning in lang uage detection and provide a foundation for
further advancements in multilingual text processing. The architecture of the proposed novel BiLSTM model
with a Multi-Head Attention mechanism is presented in Figure A.

Purpose: Language detection is a fundamental task in natural language pr ocessing (NLP), widely applied
in areas such as machine translation, sentiment analysis, and i nformation retrieval. Effective language
detection facilitates seamless communication between individual s across different linguistic backgrounds. 
This study aims to develop a novel deep learning-based approach to accurately classify text into 17 different
languages. 
 
Theory and Methods: A novel Bidirectional Long Short-Term Memory (BiLSTM) model with Multi-Head 
Attention is proposed. The model is trained on a dataset of 10, 337 text samples across 17 languages and
evaluated using accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score. The proposed model’s effectiveness is compared
against state-of-the-art baseline models. 
 
Results: The proposed model achieves 99.9% accuracy, surpassing state-of-the-art models. It attains 100% 
precision for 15 languages, demonstrating its effectiveness in multilingual text classification. 
 
Conclusion: The study highlights the potential of deep learning in language  detection, offering valuable
insights for multilingual text processing and NLP applications.
