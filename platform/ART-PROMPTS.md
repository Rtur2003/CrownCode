## Project specimens

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool. The source images are original generated PNGs. Final assets were converted to 1254 × 1254 WebP with Sharp at quality 80. The visual reference was the existing warm, dark mastering-studio photography in `public/images/showroom/crown-studio.webp` and `tape-study.webp`.

Shared direction: one physically plausible circular object, centered and large with its full edge visible, resting on a near-black `#100d0a` scratched mastering-studio table. Soft amber light comes from the upper right. Preserve natural optics, slight wear, material texture, deep shadows, and restrained film grain. No text, logos, symbols, UI, neon, cosmic scenery, floating objects, or exaggerated effects.
